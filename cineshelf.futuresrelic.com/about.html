Install guide

Works like a native app.

CineShelf is a Progressive Web App (PWA). No app store required — install directly from your browser in seconds.

🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS)

  1. Open cineshelf.ca in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome)
  2. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add — CineShelf appears on your home screen like any app

🤖 Android

  1. Open cineshelf.ca in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. Tap Install — CineShelf is added to your launcher

💻 Desktop (Mac / Windows)

  1. Open cineshelf.ca in Chrome or Edge
  2. Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar
  3. Click it and select Install
  4. CineShelf opens as a standalone window, no browser UI needed
Connected apps

Better together with UMDB.

CineShelf connects to the Universal Media Database for barcode lookups, edition metadata, and two-way sync.

🔗 UMDB Partner

Scan a barcode. Get everything.

When you scan or type a UPC or EAN barcode, CineShelf queries UMDB to automatically fill in the edition name, publisher, studio, disc count, country, language, and format. No manual entry needed for supported releases.


Your physical copies can be pushed to UMDB and become part of the community database — helping other collectors identify their editions too.
